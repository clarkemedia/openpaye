Questions people ask

What taxes come out of my pay in Ireland?

Three: Income Tax (20% up to your standard rate cut-off, 40% above it, reduced by your tax credits), the Universal Social Charge (USC, from 0.5% to 8%), and PRSI (social insurance, about 4.2–4.5%). Pension contributions and auto-enrolment also come out of your gross pay, but they are your savings, not tax.

What changed in Budget 2027?

From 1 January 2027 the standard rate cut-off rises by €2,500 (to €46,500 for a single person), the personal, employee and earned income credits rise by €125 to €2,125, the USC 2% band widens to €30,300, and the Rent Tax Credit increases to €1,150. Mortgage Interest Relief is not extended beyond 2026.

Why does PRSI change part-way through the year?

Under the Social Welfare Act, the employee PRSI rate rises every 1 October: 4.1% → 4.2% (Oct 2025), 4.35% (Oct 2026), 4.5% (Oct 2027). We apply each rate to the share of the year it is in force.

How accurate is this?

It applies the published rates, bands and credits the same way Revenue does on an annual basis, and every step is shown in "Show me the maths". Your real payslip may differ slightly if your tax credit certificate includes other credits, if you started mid-year, or if you are on emergency tax.

Is my data stored anywhere?

No. OpenPAYE has no cookies, no analytics and no trackers, and it never sends what you type anywhere. Every calculation runs in your browser. Your inputs are kept only on your own device and in the part of the page address after the #, which browsers never send to a server, so you can bookmark or share a calculation privately.

Can I host this myself?

Yes. OpenPAYE is open source and builds to a handful of static files, with no server, database or tracking. Run it with Docker, any static web host, or open the single-file build directly.
