How employer costs work in Ireland

Employers pay Class A PRSI on top of salary. In 2026 the rate is 9% if an employee earns €552 a week or less, and 11.25% above that, rising to 11.40% from 1 October. The rate applies to all of that week's pay, so a small pay rise across the threshold costs more than it looks.

From 1 January 2026, employers must also match auto-enrolment (My Future Fund) contributions: 1.5% of pay up to €80,000 for the first three years, rising to 6% by year ten.
